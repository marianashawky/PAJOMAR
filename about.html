Our Story

Where Craft Meets Design

PAJOMAR was founded on a simple belief: that the right curtains can transform a room. Not merely as decoration, but as an essential element of interior architecture — framing light, defining space and expressing personal style.

We partner with the world's finest fabric mills and employ master craftsmen who bring decades of expertise to every panel, pleat and hem. From sheer elegance to complete blackout luxury, our collections span the full spectrum of window dressing.

Today, PAJOMAR serves homeowners, interior designers and commercial clients who demand nothing less than exceptional quality and service.
